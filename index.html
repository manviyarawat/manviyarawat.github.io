The story

I'm Manviya. I studied mathematics at the University of Delhi, cracked IIT JAM with AIR 316, and came to IIT Gandhinagar for my MSc. Now, at the Centre for Creative Learning, I take maths out of the textbook and into classrooms: into kits, worksheets and students' hands.

Grades 6 to 12, one module at a time.
